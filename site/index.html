Use a display

On your computer, clone the public repo:

git clone https://github.com/Clubria/robobuilders-cyd.git
cd robobuilders-cyd
Download ZIP instead

Open that folder in your coding agent. Paste:

Read AGENTS.md. Start the Clubria display bridge.
Detect my display without flashing it.
Save the LAN bridge address over USB.
Guide me through Wi-Fi QR setup.
Keep passwords private and the bridge LAN-only.

Keep the computer awake. Plug in the display; use its 2 Wi-Fi and 3 Phone QR pages.

Without an agent

Requires Node22+, pnpm, Python3 and Chrome on a Mac.

pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
pnpm dev:clubria

Then open localhost:3088/preview. ZIP instead of Git.

Build an app
↓ App starterFork repo ↗

Open the starter in your coding agent. Paste:

Build my idea: [describe it].
Prioritize 240×320 CYD screens and 44px touch buttons.
Keep a browser preview for testing.
Help me host on HTTPS and fill in app.json.

Ready? Submit your app link or open a pull request. Public repo; no access request needed.

Hardware, speed and privacy
  • Supported prototype boards: ESP32-024 2.4″ and GUITION JC2432W328 2.8″. Other boards need verified wiring and firmware.
  • USB video has measured about 1 FPS at115200. Faster USB is experimental. Wi-Fi game samples on the larger board reached5–7 FPS; higher observations still need measurement.
  • No built-in camera or microphone. The phone scans QR codes. A recording app needs an authorized account and an actual audio source.
  • Wi-Fi passwords stay on your display. The public ZIP excludes device settings, credentials, backups and browser profiles.
  • The local bridge is unauthenticated. Trusted LAN only; do not expose port3088 to the internet.
